# Retail Sales Analytics & Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard** built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, regional trends, customer segments, and product performance.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming retail sales data into meaningful business insights through data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key performance indicators (KPIs) to support business performance monitoring and data-driven decision-making.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify high-performing products and customer segments.
- Compare regional sales performance and returned orders.
- Monitor monthly sales trends and sales targets.
- Present key business metrics through an interactive dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and reporting
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPI development
- **Data Modeling** – Organizing data and establishing relationships
- **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, KPI cards, and reports

## Key Dashboard Features

- **Sales & Profit Analysis:** Track total sales, total profit, and overall business performance.
- **Regional Performance:** Compare sales and returned orders across regions.
- **Product Analysis:** Identify the top 10 products by sales.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Evaluate performance across customer segments.
- **Monthly Sales Trends:** Monitor sales patterns over time.
- **Sales Target Analysis:** Compare actual sales against targets.
- **Interactive Drill-through:** Explore detailed regional sales, profit, product, and customer information.
- **Dynamic Reporting:** Use slicers and conditional formatting to make comparisons easier.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Return Rate
- Sales vs. Target

## Analytical Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:** Import and transform source data using Power Query.
2. **Data Modeling:** Establish relationships between relevant data tables.
3. **Measure Development:** Create DAX measures for KPIs and business calculations.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Build interactive visualizations, slicers, and drill-through pages.
5. **Business Analysis:** Examine regional performance, product sales, profitability, and monthly trends.

## Business Impact

The dashboard helps users identify sales trends, compare regional performance, evaluate product profitability, and monitor progress against targets. These insights can support informed decisions related to sales planning, product strategy, and business performance monitoring.
